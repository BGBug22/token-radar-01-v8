How a row is priced

implied value = comps × peer rate × growth tilt × maturity tilt

TermWhat it isWhere it comes from
comps10 ^ (3.0247 + 0.5223 × log10 TVL)Fitted on the 128 traded tokens in this snapshot (OLS, log-log).
peer rateShare of protocols in the same TVL band that already list a token.Five bands, 0.52 / 0.57 / 0.59 / 0.66 / 0.56 from $5M to $5B+.
growth tilt1 + clamp(7-day change, −60, +120) ÷ 100 × 0.25Bounded tilt, stated as a tilt: growth does not move the traded multiple in-sample (R² 0.02).
maturity tilt0.9 + min(age in days, 1095) ÷ 1095 × 0.2Bounded 0.9–1.1; age does not move the traded multiple in-sample either (R² 0.00).
